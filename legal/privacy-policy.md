## Overview

Seed ("we," "us," "our") is a personalized homeschool learning platform for children ages 5–13. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information about parents and their children.

## Information We Collect

### Parent Information
- Email address and name (for account management)
- Timezone (for scheduling)
- Payment information (processed by Stripe; we do not store card numbers)

### Child Information (collected with verifiable parental consent per COPPA)
- First name, age, gender/pronouns, and language preference
- Learning preferences (subjects, pedagogy lean, content axis, accommodations)
- Lesson responses, progress data, and mastery state
- Wonder questions asked during lessons
- Voice recordings (if voice answer feature is used)
- Drawings and creative work produced during lessons

## How We Use Information

All child data is used exclusively to:
- Generate personalized lesson content
- Track learning progress and mastery
- Create parent-facing reports and portfolios
- Improve lesson quality based on parent feedback

We **never** use child data for:
- Behavioral advertising
- Sale to third parties
- Profiling for non-educational purposes

## Data Retention

- Account data: retained while account is active
- Voice recordings: auto-deleted after 90 days unless parent marks as favorite
- All data: deleted upon parent request (COPPA right to deletion)

## Third-Party Services

- **Supabase**: Database and authentication (data stored in US)
- **Anthropic**: AI lesson generation (server-side only; no child PII sent)
- **Stripe**: Payment processing
- **Sentry**: Error monitoring (PII scrubbed; no child data)
- **PostHog**: Privacy-respecting analytics (parent events only; no child tracking)

## Parental Rights (COPPA)

Parents may at any time:
- Review all data collected about their child
- Export all family data as JSON
- Request deletion of a child's profile and all associated data
- Revoke consent (which will deactivate the child's profile)
